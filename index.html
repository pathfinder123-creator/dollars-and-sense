Dollars and Sense

Give your money a plan.

Install on your phone: use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option. On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After the first load, this tool is available offline.

Amounts are in US dollars. Your budget is saved in this browser on this device. Download a backup to keep a separate copy.

Clear all saved budgets?

This removes the budgets on this device. Download a backup first if you want to keep them.

Print / Save as PDF

Choose Save as PDF in your device’s print window to create a PDF.
